You **may use** the Mayan EDMS Trademarks without prior permission in the following ways:

- **Truthful, factual references**:
    - "Mayan EDMS is an enterprise open-source electronic document management system."
    - "Built with Mayan EDMS."
- **Community and educational content**:
    - Tutorials, blog posts, courses, reviews, and videos discussing or demonstrating Mayan EDMS.
- **Redistribution of the unmodified software**:
    - When you redistribute Mayan EDMS **without modification**, you may continue to identify it by its name and logo, because what you are distributing is in fact Mayan EDMS. This is so whether or not you charge a fee for the redistribution or for any service accompanying it. If you modify the software, section 5 applies instead.

When using the name, always capitalize it as **Mayan EDMS**, use the ® symbol on the first prominent use, and include a trademark notice when feasible:

## 5. Forks and Derivatives

If you create a modified version (fork) of Mayan EDMS:

- You **must not use the name "Mayan EDMS"** as the main name of your fork.
- You **must clearly state** that your project is a fork or derivative.
- You may state, "This project is based on Mayan EDMS," with proper attribution.

This protects users from confusion between the official project and derivatives.

### 5.1 What "main name" means

For the purposes of this section, the **main name** of your fork is the name by which the software identifies itself to its users, or by which you identify it to others. This includes, without limitation:

- the product or service name used in marketing, documentation, proposals, or sales material;
- the application name displayed in the user interface, including the navigation bar, the About page, and the browser or window title;
- the logo displayed in any of those locations;
- the name of the package, source repository, container image, or installer that you distribute.

Retaining the Mayan EDMS copyright notice is required of you by the software license, and is a **separate obligation** from this policy. Keeping that notice is necessary, and it does not by itself permit you to keep the Mayan EDMS name or logo as the identity of your fork. A modified version that presents itself to its users as "Mayan EDMS" does not comply with this section, however faithfully it preserves the copyright notice.
